Our Story

Traditional flavors from the Silk Road

For over two decades, Kashkar Cafe has been serving the finest Uyghur cuisine in Brooklyn. Our recipes come from family traditions passed down through generations, bringing the authentic flavors of Xinjiang to Brighton Beach.

Every dish is prepared with care—hand-pulled laghman noodles, slow-cooked stews, and freshly baked breads from our tandoor oven. We use only the freshest ingredients and traditional spices sourced directly from Central Asia.
